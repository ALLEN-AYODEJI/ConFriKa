Sign Mode, for Deaf & non-speaking users.

Lay your phone down, step back, and let ConFrika be your voice.

1

Watch yourself sign.The screen splits, your front-camera self-view below, the conversation above.

2

Sign to speak.Fingerspell or tap a phrase, and ConFrika says it out loud for the hearing person.

3

See what they say.Their speech becomes live captions, so the conversation flows both ways.

4

On calls & in meetings.Sign and your words are spoken into the call; our browser extension reads your signing live from the camera in web video calls.
